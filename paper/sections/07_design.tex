\section{Study Design}
\label{sec:design}
\label{sec:triggers}
\label{sec:ladder}

\textbf{Trigger.} Every model-consulting arm shares one frozen trigger: a proposal opportunity
opens when an alert arrives or a CUSUM on observed demand~\cite{page-cusum} crosses a threshold
set on development nulls, with a five-period refractory window and at most two firings per
episode. The trigger reads only exogenous streams, so all arms fire in the same periods and see
the same first prompt.

\textbf{Arms.} Arm~1 is the published capped base-stock policy. Arm~8 acts on a proposal at once
(the compiled configuration of the latest \shockspec{}, forever); arm~10 is the gate of
Section~\ref{sec:verify}. Certify-then-hedge runs with $\lambda = 0.25$ (the method), with
$\lambda = 0$ (the model's family only), and with $\lambda = 1$ (content-free: the same trigger,
alerts included, and machinery, with no model call). Arm~10 alone keeps the pilot's rule that
turns a proposal its lifecycle cannot register (a persistence or duration of \texttt{none}, or
one made at the horizon) into an abstention. Controls complete the ladder: a telemetry-only hidden-Markov
policy (arm~2), the detector compiled without language, a keyword parser of the alert text, the
alert's hidden specification compiled at onset minus one (a perfect-reading upper bound), and a
hidden-truth oracle.

\textbf{Models.} The proposers are called at temperature 0 with no system prompt, and every
response's echoed model name is checked (one provider silently served retired names as a newer
model). The primary models are Gemini~3.8 Flash and Grok~4.20 (non-reasoning).
The model-scale study adds 19 more: older and smaller Gemini and Grok models, four StepFun
reasoning models, and eleven open-weight or legacy models through OpenRouter (Llama~3.2 1B and
3B, 3.1 8B, 3.3 70B; Qwen~2.5 7B and 72B; Gemma~3 27B; DeepSeek-V3; gpt-oss-20b; GPT-3.5 Turbo
and GPT-4o-mini).

\textbf{Registration trail.} Every stage was committed and pushed before its outcomes were
seen, and every change after that is a dated amendment in the study plan. (i) The Gate~3 pilot,
registered with a frozen decision rule, ran the full arm ladder and its controls on 120 development and calibration
episodes (24 seeds) with scripted model content; it read \emph{kill-or-reframe}. (ii) An
exploratory pilot replayed the same 120 episodes with live Gemini and Grok answers and the real
alert bank. (iii) The model ladder ran the same episodes with the remaining models. (iv)
Certify-then-hedge was developed on those spent episodes only, where its numbers carry no
claim. (v) The confirmation, exploratory with respect to the project's preregistration, froze
the method's code by hash and registered its hypotheses, tests and evaluator in the study plan
before its live runs; the plan discloses the earlier contact with the fresh pool
(Section~\ref{sec:limitations}). (vi) A second stage, registered the same way, tested the
benchmark's two other cost ratios and noisy lead times (Section~\ref{sec:stage-d}); before
any of its model calls, an independent audit of the registration led to dated amendments that
changed no hypothesis, pool or cap.

\textbf{Fresh seeds.} The confirmation draws 48 units (eight per family) from a seed pool
disjoint from every registered pool, each replayed under the four information conditions plus
one unshocked twin, silent or with a false demand alert, balanced by family: 192 shocked and 48
null episodes (amended from 72 units before any model call, because the alert bank renders
distinct texts for only eight units per family). A unit's seed is its cluster.

\textbf{Statistics.} Contrasts are per-episode paired differences of net reward, averaged over
all 240 episodes, with seed-cluster bootstrap 95\% intervals (10{,}000 draws) and one-sided
cluster sign-flip $p$-values (100{,}000 flips). The primary family is certify-then-hedge against
the gate and against arm~1, on each primary model, with Holm's correction~\cite{holm-multitest}
at 0.05. Null safety is non-inferiority of certify-then-hedge against arm~1 on the 48 null
episodes with a margin of $-25$ per episode, and exposure is the summed per-period shock mass
$\Pi_t$ on null episodes.
