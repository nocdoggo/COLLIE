\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\textbf{Anytime-valid checks on language-model outputs.} POPPER runs
agent-designed falsification experiments under Type-I error
control~\cite{popper}; E-valuator stops failing agent trajectories with a
sequential test on verifier scores~\cite{e-valuator}; Conformal Selective
Acting certifies with Ville-type \eprocess{}es when a fine-tuned model may
act~\cite{csa}; and in Propose, Don't Judge a frozen anytime-valid referee
admits agent-proposed factors~\cite{propose-dont-judge}. All four, like our
registered gate, accept or refuse at an evidence threshold. Certify-then-hedge
hedges instead: the model only reweights a fixed, action-aligned hypothesis set,
the prior-weighted \eprocess{}es become posterior odds, and the order is a
Bayes-mixture base-stock quantile.

\textbf{Anytime-valid inference.} We take e-values and Ville's inequality as
given~\cite{vovk-wang-evalues, ramdas-savi, ville} and read an \eprocess{} as a
bettor's wealth~\cite{shafer-betting}. Universal inference builds valid e-values
for composite nulls by data splitting~\cite{universal-inference}; we use a
plug-in demand null instead, so our guarantee holds only as far as it does
(Section~\ref{sec:limitations}). Gr\"unwald's e-posterior has risk bounds that
loosen, not fail, when its e-collection, the analogue of a prior, is chosen
badly~\cite{grunwald-eposterior}, and e-values keep Type-I risk control when the
loss is chosen after the data~\cite{grunwald-beyond-np}; our hedged prior plays
that role for the language model's answer.

\textbf{Inventory under uncertain information.} Advance demand information
offsets lead time~\cite{hariharan-zipkin} and makes state-dependent base-stock
policies optimal~\cite{gallego-ozer}; with the demand law unknown, the classical
answers are Bayesian updating~\cite{scarf-bayes} and a min-max
order~\cite{scarf-minmax}. Our signal is text of unknown reliability that only
splits a fixed prior budget, and the mass that moves the order comes from
\eprocess{}es, so the order stays near arm~1 until evidence arrives.

\textbf{Language models in operations.} On InventoryBench, language models
augmented with operations research outperform either
alone~\cite{inventorybench}. Language models read event text into demand
forecasts~\cite{eventcast}, turn context into predicted disturbances for model
predictive control~\cite{instructmpc}, generate distributions for
newsvendor-type decisions~\cite{llm-saa}, and record plans as falsifiable
commitments or tracked hypotheses~\cite{fcpagent, hep}. STOCKTAKE finds a
knowing-doing gap: models detect 84 to 88\% of hidden failures, yet where stress
persists 34 to 43\% of correctly diagnosed stress weeks still end in a
stockout~\cite{stocktake}. Certify-then-hedge takes no order and no demand
distribution from the model.
