# COLING 2027: venue facts (checked on official pages, 2026-09-29)

Each fact was read on the page named, and a second reader re-opened each source.

## Route and dates

- COLING 2027, the 32nd International Conference on Computational Linguistics: Macau, main
  conference 9 to 14 May 2027 (in person only), virtual sessions 6 to 7 May.
  <https://2027.coling-iccl.org/calls/main_conference_papers/>
- **Main-conference papers come only through ACL Rolling Review.** The October 2026 cycle is the
  last one COLING 2027 takes; papers reviewed in earlier cycles can also be committed. A paper
  first submitted in January 2027 cannot go to COLING 2027's main track. (Same page.)
- ARR October 2026: submission **12 October 2026, 23:59 AoE**; reviewer registration 14 October;
  reviews due 16 November; author response 24 to 30 November; meta-reviews 17 December;
  commitment **23 December 2026**; COLING notification 10 February 2027.
  <https://aclrollingreview.org/dates>
- The October cycle is shared with NAACL 2027. The venue is chosen only at commitment, after the
  meta-reviews; the "preferred venue" on the submission form is non-binding. One primary venue,
  optionally the other as secondary (at the chairs' discretion).
  <https://2027.coling-iccl.org/faq/>
- COLING has no Findings: every acceptance is main conference. NAACL can accept to main or
  Findings. (FAQ.)
- Industry Track (separate, direct, 30 November 2026, 6 pages) requires deployed or soon-deployed
  real-world systems and forbids papers under review elsewhere: not our route.
  <https://2027.coling-iccl.org/calls/industry_track/>

## Format and rules

- Long paper: 8 pages of main body; a mandatory Limitations section after the conclusion;
  optional ethics section; unlimited references and appendix. (Main call.)
- ARR: two-way anonymous; self-citations in the third person; anonymised supplementary material
  and repositories; no anonymity period for preprints (declared on the form).
  <https://aclrollingreview.org/cfp>
- Overlap with published work: ARR will not consider a paper that "overlaps significantly in
  content or results" with published work; no overlap in stated contributions; reused text
  under 10% of tokens and marked; the prior paper disclosed, cited anonymously, with a clear
  statement of how the new paper differs. (CFP; ACL Policy on Publication Ethics.)
- Contemporaneous work: papers appearing less than three months before the deadline need not be
  compared in detail but should be cited and discussed. (CFP.)
- **Sustainable reviewing (from this cycle):** each submission names a qualified service
  contributor (at most two submissions each), or goes to a review lottery. Qualification needs at
  least two papers in major CL venues or Findings (or listed ML venues). All authors need complete
  OpenReview profiles (ORCID, affiliation history, DBLP or Anthology links) within one week of
  the deadline. <https://aclrollingreview.org/sustainable-reviewing-2026>,
  <https://aclrollingreview.org/qualifications>. The owner qualifies (ACL, ICML, NeurIPS).

## Acceptance rates (not directly comparable denominators)

- COLING 2025 main: 30.1% overall (direct long 31.5%, direct short 20.6%, ARR-committed long
  34.7%). <https://aclanthology.org/2025.coling-main.0.pdf>
- COLING 2022: 634 accepted of 2,253 submissions (28.1% of submissions; 33.4% of reviewed long
  papers). <https://coling2022.org/programme>
- NAACL 2025: about 22% main and 15% Findings of October-cycle submissions intended for NAACL.
  <https://aclanthology.org/2025.naacl-long.0.pdf>
- NAACL 2024: 23.2% main, 12.5% Findings. <https://2024.naacl.org/blog/acceptance/>
- 2027 is COLING's first ARR-only year, reviewed alongside NAACL; ARR reviewers apply one
  standard (an ACL main-conference paper), and venues differ only in their thresholds. The 2025
  rate may not carry over.
