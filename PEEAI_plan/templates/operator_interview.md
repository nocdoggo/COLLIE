## Recent concrete incident

1. Describe the last unexpected change that forced you to revise an event plan.
2. What did you know first, and where did that information arrive?
3. What did you check before acting?
4. What could you change yourself, and what needed another person's approval?
5. What was the cost of waiting? What was the cost of reacting unnecessarily?
6. Which existing tools or records were essential?
7. What did the eventual result reveal that you did not know at decision time?

## Proposed workflow walkthrough

Show one small before/after plan and a source-linked action record. Ask the participant to explain the proposed action in their own words. Ask which constraint is missing, which source they would trust for this fact, and whether the action is within their role.

For a community participant, test a check-in or resource offer. Ask whether its consequence is clear, whether they can correct it, and whether there is a usable alternative to a phone or QR code.
