\begin{column}{\colwidth}
  \begin{block}{Same loop, a new rule}
    In a fictional pantry, late stock does not count, and only staff record a handoff or a
    distribution. \textbf{No kit was counted twice} in any held-out run.
  \end{block}

  \begin{block}{What I built}
    A local server and board with a checked executor, a planner, holds, receipts, four
    policies and a frozen test harness.
  \end{block}

  \begin{block}{Limits}
    Fictional sandbox, 12 held-out cases, one model, one reported run after a voided first
    attempt. My P2 missed cases through a frozen rule, choices that used up the 8 model calls,
